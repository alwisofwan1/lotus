### New

- You can now delete a Lotus account, and remove a ccs or env account from Lotus.
- Claude panes and Claude agent nodes now show which account they use. On a pane, you can pick another account, and the pane restarts on it and picks up the same conversation.
- When a Claude pane hits its usage limit, a strip under the pane lets you continue on another account.
- ⌘K now has a single Claude row that shows the account it will start on. Press Tab or click the account to switch it.
- Code, Chat and Canvas now share one control for conversation titles. The old pane title model setting now lives in Chat › Titles, and conversation titles have a new dithered look.
- After a canvas node's first reply, a model now improves the node's title.
- Node headers no longer show the runner's name. The logo shows which runner it is.

### Improved

- Lotus does less background work while agents are printing output.

### Fixed

- The agent-done sound now plays natively.
- The main window no longer reloads its page after it first loads.
- The pane account picker and the pane title menu no longer open behind the terminal.
- The title model setting now names every mode, not only Chat.
- Switching a pane's account now restarts it on the account you picked, instead of keeping the old one.
- A pane no longer goes blank after you switch its account.
- While a pane restarts on another account, it is now covered and holds your keystrokes until it is ready.
- New canvas nodes now get a title, and your first message to them is no longer lost.
