### New

- Frames: create, rename and fill Frames from the rail, and open one by clicking its row. Each row shows its status and a stack of agent logos. Frames are saved and restored with your workspace, and you can find them in ⌘K.
- A Frame menu lets you pick a colour or delete the Frame, with Undo.
- Splits and groups: you can add a new pane without first making a Frame. Groups use pastel colours and show a row of stacked agent logos.
- Click anywhere on a group's header to fold or unfold it, with an animation.
- When an agent opens a new pane, that pane joins the agent's group.
- Right-click a pane's header to open its menu.
- Drag a pane by its header onto a group in the rail, or drag panes between groups there. Drop a pane's header on another pane to swap the two.
- Jump palette: on a Mac, press ⌘J from any mode to open it.
- Needs you: one list in the Jump palette of everything that is waiting for you.
- Panes in the rail show a working halo while their agent works. All halos pulse together, and a pane gets a finish signal when its agent is done.
- Add review notes on the Changes diff.
- See inline blame at the end of the line your cursor is on.
- Managed Host: install and update the Lotus host on a remote machine over SSH.

### Fixed

- Reloading a diff no longer clears a review note you are still typing.
- Type names now get their colour in the diff and in the Files preview.
- The gold chat colour now shows on every chat surface.
- A pane no longer shows turn activity from agents that are not its own.
- A squad opened in a Frame now appears as one balanced block.
- Built-in browser views no longer stay on screen when you switch away from their mode.
